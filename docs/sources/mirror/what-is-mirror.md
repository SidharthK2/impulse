Mirror is a high-performance perps exchange built from scratch for markets that have never existed before, powered by a new technology we developed called MicroPerps.
Imagine if you could buy into any Seed or Series A startup backed by big-name Venture Capitalists like Sequoia or a16z, from the moment their funding round was announced. Why isn't this possible today?
Many don't know this, but modern perps exchanges can only list perpetuals for the most liquid assets that exist. They cannot support the permissionless listing of less-liquid long-tail assets like early-stage startups, prediction markets, or even memecoins because their risk management systems aren't designed to handle them. Even after listing an asset, if that asset's liquidity subsequently deteriorates, then they have to delist it. ## Introducing MicroPerps
MicroPerps re-imagine the risk mechanics of modern perps from the ground up to support the long tail of less-liquid assets, while also being capable of seamlessly powering the most liquid assets in the world as well (i.e. a no-tradeoffs expansion of what assets perps can represent). This can allow us to go from perps on hundreds of assets maintained by gatekeepers to perps on millions of assets maintained by no one, making perps permissionless in the original sense of the word. It also allows for the creation of whole new asset categories for perps that couldn't exist with prior models.

## Fee Structure
Perhaps the best part: 100% of trading fees collected go back to token-holders via a buy & burn mechanism. 90% of fees go to buy and burn $MIRROR, the exchange's native token, which anyone can access before we launch, while the remaining 10% goes to buy and burn $DESO to promote the proliferation of decentralized social media. There is no private round and no venture capitalist involvement prior to the public sale. ## Why Now?
Today, perps are exploding in adoption. In spite of this, everything you trade on Binance or Hyperliquid has to go through a centralized listing process run by gatekeepers, who are furthermore risk-constrained in what they can let you trade. With Mirror you'll be able to trade any asset that exists, with fees going back to actual users of the platform. Now isn't that the ideal endgame of DeFi?
